# Privacy Policy for PaperKnife Media Tools

**Last Updated: September 2026**

### 1. On-Device Local Processing
PaperKnife operates completely on-device. All image, PDF, audio, and video transformations are executed locally on your device. Your files, media, and document contents are never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

### 2. Information Collection & Storage
- **Local Activity Log**: Processed document metadata (file names, sizes, output timestamps) is stored locally in your device's internal database for the Activity Clipboard feature.
- **Local Settings**: Preferences such as theme choices, haptic toggles, and metadata defaults are stored locally on your device via DataStore.
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not include third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

### 3. Data Retention & Erasure
You have full control over your data. You can clear your activity clipboard or perform a complete data reset ("Delete All Data") at any time within System Preferences.

### 4. Permissions
The app requests access to device storage, photos, audio, and video solely to allow you to select and transform files.
